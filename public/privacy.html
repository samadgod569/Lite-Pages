Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

We collect only what we need to run Fast Site. We don’t sell your data.

1. What we collect

Your account

  • From Discord when you sign in: your Discord ID, username, email address and avatar.
  • Your IP address at sign-up, the dates you joined and last logged in, and your credit balance.
  • Your password, which we store only in a hashed form. Your username and password are also kept in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in on that device. App passwords are stored hashed and encrypted.

Your apps

  • Files, KV and S3 data, backups, settings, domains, team members and invites.

Visitors to sites hosted on Fast Site

  • When someone opens a hosted site we log the time, page, IP address, approximate location (country, region, city, postal code, coordinates), time zone, and device, OS and browser. This powers the app’s stats and helps us stop abuse.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account and apps, enforce plan limits and keep the service secure.
  • To show app owners and their team the stats of their own site.
  • To prevent abuse, fix problems and meet legal duties.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes.

3. Who sees it

  • App owners and team members can see the stats and files of the apps they belong to. Team members see the other members’ usernames.
  • Service providers that run Fast Site, such as Discord (sign-in) and our hosting and storage providers, process data for us.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.
  • We never sell your personal data.

4. If you host a site here

You decide what your own site does with visitors’ data. You are responsible for your site’s own privacy notice and legal duties. Fast Site’s visitor logs described above are collected by us in addition to whatever your site does.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in, remember app passwords on your device and remember your theme. We don’t use it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

  • Detailed visit logs are kept for a short time (about 7 days), after which they are deleted or reduced to totals.
  • Account data is kept while your account is open.
  • When an app is deleted, its files, KV and S3 data, backups and team records are removed. Some copies in provider backups may remain for a short time.
  • To delete your account and its data, email us at the address below.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, fix, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have more rights, and you can complain to your local data authority. Email us and we’ll reply within a reasonable time.

8. Security

We use hashed and encrypted storage and limit who can access data. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong, unique password and keep your app passwords private.

9. Children

Fast Site is not for children under 13. If you think a child gave us data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in other countries. We take reasonable steps to protect it wherever it is processed.
